About

Born in the city of Teresina, in northeastern Brazil and now currently a computer science student at Universidade Federal do Piauí, interested in how systems are built and scaled, and sometime also a bit of maths.

My main focus is backend engineering, cloud infrastructure, and security. And lately, I’ve been spending more time studying networks, security and specifically Spring Boot more in-depth. That includes protocols, common misconfigurations, attack surfaces, and how small design decisions can turn into serious problems in production systems.

My interests outside of technology are strongly tied to atmosphere, aesthetics, and how environments influence perception and behavior.

I’m particularly drawn to late-90s and early-2000s cinema, where urban loneliness, artificial light, and analog imperfections play a central role in storytelling.

Films and series like After Hours, Miracle Mile, Chungking Express and Lain portray cities as living systems, chaotic, fragmented and emotionally dense.

This same aesthetic appears in my relationship with music. I tend to gravitate toward sounds that emphasize texture and atmosphere, from electronic and experimental pop to alternative rock and reggae.

Artists like Daft Punk, Dennis Brown, Lô Borges, Mac DeMarco, and Tyler, The Creator represent a strong sense of authorship, while bands such as Beach House, Cage The Elephant and Crystal Castles create immersive spaces that feel introspective, distorted or deliberately imperfect.

This site is a personal space. Here I write notes, document experiments, and keep track of things I’m learning or just write about something that I like.
